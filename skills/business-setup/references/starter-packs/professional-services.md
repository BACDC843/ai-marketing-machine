# Starter pack — Professional services

**Typical for the trade. Not yet confirmed by the owner.** Every line below is a
starting point drawn from how businesses like this usually work — it is not a
claim about *this* business. Seed it, then confirm or correct it in the
interview, and mark what the owner actually confirms.

Anything still carrying `[starter-pack default]` when setup finishes is
unconfirmed, and `doctor` will report it as such. That is fine and honest; a
silently generic profile is not.

Covers: accountants, solicitors, consultants, bookkeepers, agencies, IFAs.

---

## `context/audience.md`

**## Who every piece of content is written for**
[starter-pack default] Business owners and decision-makers who know they need this and are choosing between providers.

**## Mindset, pain points, desires**
- **Mindset:** [starter-pack default] Comparing. Assumes competence is table stakes and is really judging whether they'd want to talk to you monthly.
- **Pain points:** [starter-pack default] Can't evaluate technical quality from outside. Worried about hidden fees, or about being sold something they don't need.
- **Desires:** [starter-pack default] Someone who explains things without condescension and answers the phone.
- **Decision triggers:** [starter-pack default] A deadline, a change in circumstances, or a bad experience with the incumbent.

**## Where they are**
[starter-pack default] LinkedIn usually leads here — genuinely, not by default. Email is often stronger than social. Instagram rarely justifies the effort.

---

## `context/brand-voice.md`

**## Voice pillars** — starting points only. The interview's tone and wedge answers must override these.
[starter-pack default] Authoritative and plain. Explains rather than impresses. Jargon only where it is the precise word, never as a credential.

Do **not** seed `## Strong opinion / wedge` or `## Recent stories worth telling`. The wedge has to come from the owner or it is worthless, and inventing a story is forbidden outright. Leave the wedge as "Not yet captured" until asked.

---

## `context/products.md`

**## Constraints and honesty flags**
[starter-pack default] Regulated sectors have strict rules on advertising, claims, and disclaimers. Ask what body regulates them and what wording is required — this is not optional.

Do **not** seed offerings or pricing. Those are business-specific and guessing at them is exactly the failure this product is built to avoid.

## `context/style-guides/social.md`

**## Content pillars** — the highest-value thing this pack supplies.
1. [starter-pack default] The question clients keep asking — answered properly
2. [starter-pack default] What changed — regulation, deadlines, rates, and what it means in practice
3. [starter-pack default] How we work — process, what engagement actually looks like
4. [starter-pack default] The cost of getting it wrong — specific, not scaremongering
5. [starter-pack default] Who we're for, and who we aren't

**## Platforms that actually matter**
[starter-pack default] LinkedIn usually leads here — genuinely, not by default. Email is often stronger than social. Instagram rarely justifies the effort.

## `context/style-guides/lead-magnets.md`

**## Is a downloadable actually the right offer?**
[starter-pack default] Downloadables genuinely work here — checklists, deadline calendars, 'what to bring to your first meeting'. This is the trade where a gated asset earns its place.
