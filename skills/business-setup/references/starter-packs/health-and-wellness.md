# Starter pack — Health & wellness

**Typical for the trade. Not yet confirmed by the owner.** Every line below is a
starting point drawn from how businesses like this usually work — it is not a
claim about *this* business. Seed it, then confirm or correct it in the
interview, and mark what the owner actually confirms.

Anything still carrying `[starter-pack default]` when setup finishes is
unconfirmed, and `doctor` will report it as such. That is fine and honest; a
silently generic profile is not.

Covers: clinics, dentists, physios, gyms, therapists, salons, opticians.

---

## `context/audience.md`

**## Who every piece of content is written for**
[starter-pack default] People with a specific concern, often one they've put off addressing.

**## Mindset, pain points, desires**
- **Mindset:** [starter-pack default] Often anxious or embarrassed. The barrier is rarely price; it's the appointment itself.
- **Pain points:** [starter-pack default] Doesn't know what will happen, whether it will hurt, whether they'll be judged.
- **Desires:** [starter-pack default] To feel it will be handled kindly and competently.
- **Decision triggers:** [starter-pack default] Pain, a milestone, or a photograph they didn't like.

**## Where they are**
[starter-pack default] Instagram and Facebook both matter; Instagram for results and atmosphere, Facebook for the older end and for reviews.

---

## `context/brand-voice.md`

**## Voice pillars** — starting points only. The interview's tone and wedge answers must override these.
[starter-pack default] Reassuring, never salesy. Names the fear directly. Avoids before-and-afters that shame the 'before'.

Do **not** seed `## Strong opinion / wedge` or `## Recent stories worth telling`. The wedge has to come from the owner or it is worthless, and inventing a story is forbidden outright. Leave the wedge as "Not yet captured" until asked.

---

## `context/products.md`

**## Constraints and honesty flags**
[starter-pack default] Health claims are heavily regulated in most jurisdictions. Never state or imply a treatment outcome without confirming what may legally be claimed. This is the highest-risk trade in this set.

Do **not** seed offerings or pricing. Those are business-specific and guessing at them is exactly the failure this product is built to avoid.

---

## `context/style-guides/social.md`

**## Content pillars** — the highest-value thing this pack supplies.
1. [starter-pack default] What to expect — reducing the fear of the first appointment
2. [starter-pack default] Education — what the condition or treatment actually is
3. [starter-pack default] The team — faces, qualifications, why they do this
4. [starter-pack default] Aftercare and results — what happens next
5. [starter-pack default] Myths worth correcting

**## Platforms that actually matter**
[starter-pack default] Instagram and Facebook both matter; Instagram for results and atmosphere, Facebook for the older end and for reviews.

---

## `context/style-guides/ads.md`

**## Is paid an active channel?**
[starter-pack default] Often active — this is one of the trades where paid social genuinely converts. Confirm anyway.

---

## `context/style-guides/lead-magnets.md`

**## Is a downloadable actually the right offer?**
[starter-pack default] A consultation is usually the real offer. Downloads work for education ('what to expect at your first visit').

---

## `context/style-guides/visuals.md`

**## AI-generated imagery**
[starter-pack default] Real people, real premises. Consent matters enormously — never use a patient or client image without explicit written permission, and never imply a result is typical when it isn't.

## Not seeded, deliberately

- **`brand-board.md`** — never invent hex codes, fonts, or a logo. Colours come from the website scrape or from the owner.
- **`blog.md` target keywords** — leave empty rather than guessing.
- **`brand-voice.md` recent stories** — never invent an entry.
- **Offerings and pricing** — business-specific by definition.
