# Starter pack — Retail & e-commerce

**Typical for the trade. Not yet confirmed by the owner.** Every line below is a
starting point drawn from how businesses like this usually work — it is not a
claim about *this* business. Seed it, then confirm or correct it in the
interview, and mark what the owner actually confirms.

Anything still carrying `[starter-pack default]` when setup finishes is
unconfirmed, and `doctor` will report it as such. That is fine and honest; a
silently generic profile is not.

Covers: shops, makers, online stores, boutiques, craftspeople.

---

## `context/audience.md`

**## Who every piece of content is written for**
[starter-pack default] People who buy on taste as much as need, and who follow brands they like.

**## Mindset, pain points, desires**
- **Mindset:** [starter-pack default] Browsing rather than searching. Discovery-led — they weren't looking for this today.
- **Pain points:** [starter-pack default] Can't judge quality or size online. Worried about returns and about it looking different in person.
- **Desires:** [starter-pack default] To find something they wouldn't have found otherwise.
- **Decision triggers:** [starter-pack default] A new drop, a gift occasion, a discount, or simply seeing it.

**## Where they are**
[starter-pack default] Instagram leads, Facebook for a slightly older customer, Pinterest genuinely matters for some products and is worth asking about.

---

## `context/brand-voice.md`

**## Voice pillars** — starting points only. The interview's tone and wedge answers must override these.
[starter-pack default] Enthusiastic without hype. Specific about materials and making. Lets the product be the reason.

Do **not** seed `## Strong opinion / wedge` or `## Recent stories worth telling`. The wedge has to come from the owner or it is worthless, and inventing a story is forbidden outright. Leave the wedge as "Not yet captured" until asked.

---

## `context/products.md`

**## Constraints and honesty flags**
[starter-pack default] Pricing, delivery, and returns terms must be accurate wherever they appear.

Do **not** seed offerings or pricing. Those are business-specific and guessing at them is exactly the failure this product is built to avoid.

## `context/style-guides/social.md`

**## Content pillars** — the highest-value thing this pack supplies.
1. [starter-pack default] New in — what just arrived
2. [starter-pack default] How it's made — process, materials, the maker
3. [starter-pack default] Styled and in use — the product in a real context
4. [starter-pack default] Behind the counter — the business as a business
5. [starter-pack default] Customer-owned — real people with the product, with permission

**## Platforms that actually matter**
[starter-pack default] Instagram leads, Facebook for a slightly older customer, Pinterest genuinely matters for some products and is worth asking about.
