# Starter pack — Food & hospitality

**Typical for the trade. Not yet confirmed by the owner.** Every line below is a
starting point drawn from how businesses like this usually work — it is not a
claim about *this* business. Seed it, then confirm or correct it in the
interview, and mark what the owner actually confirms.

Anything still carrying `[starter-pack default]` when setup finishes is
unconfirmed, and `doctor` will report it as such. That is fine and honest; a
silently generic profile is not.

Covers: cafes, restaurants, bakeries, caterers, bars, food trucks.

---

## `context/audience.md`

**## Who every piece of content is written for**
[starter-pack default] Local people within a short travel radius, plus visitors deciding where to eat today.

**## Mindset, pain points, desires**
- **Mindset:** [starter-pack default] Deciding now, often on a phone, often hungry. Low patience for anything that isn't a picture of food or an opening time.
- **Pain points:** [starter-pack default] Doesn't know if it'll be busy, whether to book, whether there's anything they can eat.
- **Desires:** [starter-pack default] To know it's good and that they'll get in.
- **Decision triggers:** [starter-pack default] Time of day. Weather. Something that looks good in a feed.

**## Where they are**
[starter-pack default] Instagram first — this is one of the few trades where that is genuinely true rather than a default. Facebook for events and opening hours. TikTok if there's appetite for video.

---

## `context/brand-voice.md`

**## Voice pillars** — starting points only. The interview's tone and wedge answers must override these.
[starter-pack default] Warm and immediate. Present tense. Short. Food does the work; the caption gets out of the way.

Do **not** seed `## Strong opinion / wedge` or `## Recent stories worth telling`. The wedge has to come from the owner or it is worthless, and inventing a story is forbidden outright. Leave the wedge as "Not yet captured" until asked.

---

## `context/products.md`

**## Constraints and honesty flags**
[starter-pack default] Allergen information must be accurate wherever it appears. Never let content imply a dish is free of something without confirmation.

Do **not** seed offerings or pricing. Those are business-specific and guessing at them is exactly the failure this product is built to avoid.

---

## `context/style-guides/social.md`

**## Content pillars** — the highest-value thing this pack supplies.
1. [starter-pack default] What's on today — specials, seasonal changes, what just came out of the oven
2. [starter-pack default] The making of it — hands, process, early mornings
3. [starter-pack default] The people — staff, regulars, suppliers
4. [starter-pack default] The room — atmosphere at different times of day
5. [starter-pack default] Where it comes from — suppliers, provenance, why this ingredient

**## Platforms that actually matter**
[starter-pack default] Instagram first — this is one of the few trades where that is genuinely true rather than a default. Facebook for events and opening hours. TikTok if there's appetite for video.

---

## `context/style-guides/ads.md`

**## Is paid an active channel?**
[starter-pack default] Sometimes active, usually small and local. Confirm.

## `context/style-guides/lead-magnets.md`

**## Is a downloadable actually the right offer?**
[starter-pack default] Rarely a download. A loyalty offer, a booking, or an email list for events is the real conversion.

---

## `context/style-guides/visuals.md`

**## AI-generated imagery**
[starter-pack default] Real photography, always, and natural light. Generated food imagery reads as fake instantly to anyone who eats.
