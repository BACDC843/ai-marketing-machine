# Starter pack — Property

**Typical for the trade. Not yet confirmed by the owner.** Every line below is a
starting point drawn from how businesses like this usually work — it is not a
claim about *this* business. Seed it, then confirm or correct it in the
interview, and mark what the owner actually confirms.

Anything still carrying `[starter-pack default]` when setup finishes is
unconfirmed, and `doctor` will report it as such. That is fine and honest; a
silently generic profile is not.

Covers: estate agents, lettings, developers, architects, surveyors.

---

## `context/audience.md`

**## Who every piece of content is written for**
[starter-pack default] People moving, letting, or building — a high-value, low-frequency, high-anxiety decision.

**## Mindset, pain points, desires**
- **Mindset:** [starter-pack default] Researching heavily. Comparing agents on trust more than fee.
- **Pain points:** [starter-pack default] Doesn't know what a property is really worth, or whether they're being managed rather than represented.
- **Desires:** [starter-pack default] Straight answers and someone who returns calls.
- **Decision triggers:** [starter-pack default] A life change — job, family, separation, inheritance.

**## Where they are**
[starter-pack default] Facebook and Instagram both matter. Local Facebook groups carry real weight.

---

## `context/brand-voice.md`

**## Voice pillars** — starting points only. The interview's tone and wedge answers must override these.
[starter-pack default] Knowledgeable and local. Specific about streets and areas. Avoids 'stunning' and 'must-see', which read as estate-agent noise.

Do **not** seed `## Strong opinion / wedge` or `## Recent stories worth telling`. The wedge has to come from the owner or it is worthless, and inventing a story is forbidden outright. Leave the wedge as "Not yet captured" until asked.

---

## `context/products.md`

**## Constraints and honesty flags**
[starter-pack default] Property advertising carries specific legal requirements on descriptions, measurements, and material information. Ask what applies.

Do **not** seed offerings or pricing. Those are business-specific and guessing at them is exactly the failure this product is built to avoid.

**## Content pillars** — the highest-value thing this pack supplies.
1. [starter-pack default] New instructions and what makes each one interesting
2. [starter-pack default] The area — what it's actually like to live there
3. [starter-pack default] Process — what happens between offer and keys
4. [starter-pack default] Market reality — honest, local, specific
5. [starter-pack default] Completed projects and the detail behind them

**## Platforms that actually matter**
[starter-pack default] Facebook and Instagram both matter. Local Facebook groups carry real weight.
