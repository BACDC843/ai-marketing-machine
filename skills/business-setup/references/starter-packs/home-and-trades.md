# Starter pack — Home & trades

**Typical for the trade. Not yet confirmed by the owner.** Every line below is a
starting point drawn from how businesses like this usually work — it is not a
claim about *this* business. Seed it, then confirm or correct it in the
interview, and mark what the owner actually confirms.

Anything still carrying `[starter-pack default]` when setup finishes is
unconfirmed, and `doctor` will report it as such. That is fine and honest; a
silently generic profile is not.

Covers: plumbers, electricians, builders, roofers, landscapers, decorators, remodellers.

---

## `context/audience.md`

**## Who every piece of content is written for**
[starter-pack default] Homeowners, typically 35-65, who own the property and are spending their own money on it.

**## Mindset, pain points, desires**
- **Mindset:** [starter-pack default] Cautious. Has been let down by a trade before, or knows someone who has.
- **Pain points:** [starter-pack default] Can't tell why two quotes for the same job differ by hundreds. Worried about being overcharged, or about work starting and stopping. No way to judge quality before committing.
- **Desires:** [starter-pack default] Someone who turns up when they said, explains the price, and finishes.
- **Decision triggers:** [starter-pack default] Something has failed or is visibly getting worse. A deadline — selling, a birth, winter.

**## Where they are**
[starter-pack default] Facebook usually outperforms Instagram here — the customer skews older and local Facebook groups drive real referrals. Instagram matters for finished-work photography. Rarely LinkedIn.

---

## `context/brand-voice.md`

**## Voice pillars** — starting points only. The interview's tone and wedge answers must override these.
[starter-pack default] Plainspoken and specific. Explains the reason behind a price rather than defending it. Avoids 'quality workmanship' and 'affordable' — every competitor says both, so neither carries information.

Do **not** seed `## Strong opinion / wedge` or `## Recent stories worth telling`. The wedge has to come from the owner or it is worthless, and inventing a story is forbidden outright. Leave the wedge as "Not yet captured" until asked.

---

## `context/products.md`

**## Constraints and honesty flags**
[starter-pack default] Some trades carry licence or registration numbers that must appear on advertising. Ask.

Do **not** seed offerings or pricing. Those are business-specific and guessing at them is exactly the failure this product is built to avoid.

---

## `context/style-guides/social.md`

**## Content pillars** — the highest-value thing this pack supplies.
1. [starter-pack default] Finished work — before and after, with the detail that shows the standard
2. [starter-pack default] How it actually works — process, timelines, what happens on day one
3. [starter-pack default] Why prices differ — the single most useful education topic in this trade
4. [starter-pack default] The team — who turns up, why that matters
5. [starter-pack default] Problems spotted early — what a small issue looks like before it's expensive

**## Platforms that actually matter**
[starter-pack default] Facebook usually outperforms Instagram here — the customer skews older and local Facebook groups drive real referrals. Instagram matters for finished-work photography. Rarely LinkedIn.

---

## `context/style-guides/ads.md`

**## Is paid an active channel?**
[starter-pack default] Usually not active. Most work comes from referral and local search. Confirm before proposing any budget.

---

## `context/style-guides/lead-magnets.md`

**## Is a downloadable actually the right offer?**
[starter-pack default] A free quote or site visit is the real lead magnet. A downloadable should point at booking one, not compete with it. A 'what to ask before you hire' checklist works because it makes the reader better at judging trades — including you.

---

## `context/style-guides/visuals.md`

**## AI-generated imagery**
[starter-pack default] Real job photography is essential. A generated image of a kitchen that isn't a kitchen they built would undermine the whole proposition.
